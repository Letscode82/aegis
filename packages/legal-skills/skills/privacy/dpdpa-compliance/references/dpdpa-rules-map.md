## Third Schedule (erasure on inactivity)

| Class of fiduciary | Threshold (registered users in India) | Period |
|---|---|---|
| E-commerce entity | ≥ 2 crore | 3 years from last approach / exercise of rights, or commencement if later |
| Online gaming intermediary | ≥ 50 lakh | as above |
| Social media intermediary | ≥ 2 crore | as above |

Inform the principal at least 48 hours before erasure that it will occur unless they log in / contact the fiduciary. Exceptions: account access, virtual tokens/wallets - check the Schedule text.
