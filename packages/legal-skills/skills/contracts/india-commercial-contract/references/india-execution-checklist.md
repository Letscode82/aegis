# India Signing-Day and Cure Checklist

Use with `contracts/india-commercial-contract`. Rates and articles are never quoted from memory — look up the state schedule (`IN-STAMP-01`, [verify current]).

## A. Before signature

| # | Check | Evidence to file in AEGIS |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Exact legal names, CIN/LLPIN, registered addresses match MCA master data | MCA extract |
| 2 | Signatory authority: board resolution or PoA naming the person and the transaction; PoA itself properly stamped | Certified true copy |
| 3 | Related-party transaction? Approval under Companies Act 2013 s.188 and (if listed) SEBI LODR Reg. 23 | Audit committee / board minutes |
| 4 | Instrument classification for stamp duty (agreement, bond, guarantee, indemnity, lease, PoA) — each separately | Stamp note |
| 5 | State of execution for each party, and whether original will be "brought into" another state | Signing log |
| 6 | E-stamp certificate (SHCIL or state portal) or franking obtained in the first signatory's name, dated on or before execution | e-stamp certificate |
| 7 | Document excluded from e-signature (IT Act First Schedule)? → wet ink | — |
| 8 | Registration needed (immovable property, lease > 1 year)? → appointment with Sub-Registrar within 4 months | — |
| 9 | Counterparty MSME (Udyam) status recorded → payment terms ≤ 45 days | Udyam certificate |
| 10 | Foreign counterparty: FEMA route, AD bank documentation, withholding-tax clause, TRC | Bank advice |
| 11 | Government / PSU counterparty: Article 299 form, procurement compliance, arbitration appointment mechanism | — |

## B. Execution mechanics

- Wet ink: every page initialled; stamp paper forms first page or is annexed and referenced; date and place of signing written by each signatory.
- E-sign (Aadhaar e-sign or DSC under IT Act s.3A and Second Schedule): retain the platform audit trail, certificate details, IP/time logs; prepare BSA 2023 s.63 certificate when producing in court.
- Counterparts: confirm each counterpart is stamped or that duty is paid on the original and duplicates attract duplicate-rate duty under the state schedule.

## C. Cure plan for executed contracts (unstamped or under-stamped)

1. Identify the deficiency per state schedule.
2. Apply to the Collector for adjudication (Stamp Act s.31 or state equivalent) or pay voluntarily before production in any proceeding.
3. Pay deficit plus penalty (state acts set the multiple; some allow reduced penalty for voluntary payment — [verify current]).
4. Obtain endorsement (s.42) so the instrument becomes admissible.
5. Do this before filing suit or commencing arbitration where the instrument is the foundation of the claim; under 2023 INSC 1066 the tribunal can decide admissibility, but an unstamped instrument still cannot be acted on until cured.

## D. State acts (non-exhaustive — which act governs)

Several states have their own stamp statutes or substantial amendments: Maharashtra Stamp Act 1958, Karnataka Stamp Act 1957, Gujarat Stamp Act 1958, Kerala Stamp Act 1959, Rajasthan Stamp Act 1998, Madhya Pradesh / Tamil Nadu / Uttar Pradesh / West Bengal and Delhi apply the Indian Stamp Act 1899 with state amendments. Always confirm current schedule and e-stamp availability from the state revenue department.

## E. Dispute-ready file

Keep together: stamped original (or certified copy), stamp certificates, authority documents, signing log, e-sign audit trail, s.63 BSA certificate draft, correspondence showing performance, invoices, and the s.12A mediation record if a commercial suit is contemplated.
