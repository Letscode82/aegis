Used by `contracts/vendor-due-diligence`. "Self" = vendor self-declaration accepted. "Evidence" = document or independent source required. "—" = not required.

## Red flags that override tier (always escalate)

- Request to pay to a third party, a different country, cash, or a personal account.
- Success fee or commission linked to government approvals.
- Refusal to disclose UBOs or a structure with nominee shareholders in secrecy jurisdictions without explanation.
- Vendor recommended or insisted on by a government official or a customer's employee.
- Recent incorporation with no trading history for a high-value contract.
- Adverse media on fraud, bribery, forced labour, sanctions evasion or data breach in the last 5 years.
- Pricing materially above or below market without explanation.
